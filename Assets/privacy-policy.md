# Privacy Policy for PyNET Platform

**Effective Date:** November 2026

PyNET Platform respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information you may provide while using the application. This Privacy Policy explains what data, if any, is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

The controller of the data described here is RAEN Digital Tools SL (Spain), contact: info@raendt.com.

## 1. Data Collection and Use

The PyNET Platform plugins **do not collect, transmit, or store any personal or confidential information** from users. All scripts executed within PyNET run locally on the user's computer. No user behavior, files, or project data is transmitted to external servers or third-party services by the plugins.

The only exception is the optional **PyNET Cloud** service, described in section 3.

## 2. AI Integration Disclaimer

PyNET Platform can be connected to external AI providers (such as Claude, OpenAI, etc.) through the MCP protocol. When using AI integration, scripts and model data may be transmitted to the AI provider selected by the user. **This data exchange is governed by the AI provider's own privacy policy and terms of service**, not by PyNET Platform. Users are responsible for reviewing and accepting the privacy policies of their chosen AI provider.

## 3. PyNET Cloud

PyNET Cloud is an optional service that organisations enable for their users. It only processes data when a user chooses **Connect to cloud**.

* **Sign-in:** Users sign in with their Microsoft work account through Microsoft Entra ID. RAEN receives the account identifier, the organisation (tenant) identifier and the display name from Microsoft. Passwords are never seen or stored by PyNET.
* **What the service records:** to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service, it records technical logs of each request: the account and organisation identifiers, the script or button requested, the date and time, and the result. Project models and files are never sent to PyNET Cloud.
* **On the user's computer:** the sign-in token is kept in memory only and is discarded when the application closes. Cloud scripts are executed in memory and are not saved to disk.
* **Where:** the service runs on Microsoft Azure in the European Union. Microsoft acts as a processor for RAEN.
* **Retention:** technical logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.
* **Legal basis:** the performance of the contract with the customer organisation and RAEN's legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

## 4. Licensing and Payments

PyNET Platform uses **Freemius** as its licensing and payment platform. When purchasing a license, Freemius collects the information necessary to process the transaction (e.g., email address, payment details via Stripe). This data is handled by Freemius under their own privacy policy: [https://freemius.com/privacy/](https://freemius.com/privacy/)

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

PyNET Platform does not share any user information with third parties, including analytics tools, advertising networks, or SDK providers. The only third parties involved are those initiated by the user (AI providers), the licensing platform (Freemius) and, for PyNET Cloud, Microsoft as infrastructure and identity provider.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Outside PyNET Cloud, no user data is stored by PyNET Platform or its publisher. PyNET Cloud logs are deleted after 30 days, as described in section 3.

## 7. User Rights

PyNET Cloud users may request access to, rectification or erasure of their data, or object to its processing, by writing to info@raendt.com. They may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). Users may uninstall the application at any time to terminate usage.

## 8. In-App Access

A copy of this Privacy Policy is available within the PyNET Platform application via the **"Privacy Policy"** menu, ensuring that users can always access this information directly from the software.
